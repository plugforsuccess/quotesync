① Termination Report — prior full month
Date range to export: the entire previous calendar month (1st → last day). E.g., uploading in June → pull May 1–May 31.
In the app: set the "Report Month" picker to that month before uploading (it defaults to the current month, so you have to change it back one — this is the field people most often get wrong).
Due window: 1st–5th of the month; flagged overdue after the 5th. The checklist specifically looks for a committed upload whose report_month = prior month.

② Cancellation Audit Report
Coverage: current snapshot at time of upload (no month field — the app just records when you uploaded and checks "uploaded this month").
Due window: 8th–10th; overdue after the 10th.
Purpose: advances/closes staged cases against the terminations from ①, which is why it goes second.

③ Pending Cancellation Report
Coverage: a point-in-time snapshot ("upload current snapshot") — pull it as of today, not a date range.
Due window: twice a month — 8th–10th and 20th–25th. (This is the only report on a twice-monthly cadence.)

④ Renewal Report ("Upcoming Renewals")
Coverage: current snapshot of upcoming renewals at upload time (no month field).
Due window: 8th–10th; overdue after the 10th.

⑤ Cross-Sell Audit Report
Coverage: current snapshot at upload time.
Due window: 8th–12th; overdue after the 12th.
