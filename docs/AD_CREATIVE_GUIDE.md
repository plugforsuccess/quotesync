## Allstate Compliance Note

All ad copy and landing pages must comply with Allstate's marketing guidelines. Cameron should review all creative with his Allstate field sales leader before launching. Key rules typically include:

- Must identify as an Allstate Exclusive Agent
- Cannot guarantee savings amounts (use "typically save" or "could save" language)
- Must include appropriate disclaimers
- Cannot disparage other carriers by name in ads
