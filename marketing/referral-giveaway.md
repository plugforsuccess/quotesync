# Referral Giveaway — Marketing Copy

Reusable, compliance-aware copy for the monthly referral giveaway across
Instagram, Facebook, and internal book (existing customers).

> **DRAFT — pending legal review.** The legal boilerplate below is a working
> draft. Counsel must approve the official rules and the insurance
> anti-rebating language before any of this is used publicly. Keep the
> "refer / when we quote them" framing — never "buy a policy and win."

Placeholders are in `[brackets]`. Domain assumes `insuredbycam.com`.

## A. Short disclaimer — captions / SMS (tight space)

One winner selected at random monthly. Prize paid by Zelle/ACH; taxes are the winner's responsibility. Void where prohibited. Full official rules and free alternate method of entry: insuredbycam.com/giveaway/rules. This promotion is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Meta, Instagram, or Facebook.

## Notes on use

- **`$[X]` jackpot:** the `/giveaway` page shows the live number. In static
  posts either say "up to $5,000" (always safe) or update `[X]` from the page
  when posting.
- **Anti-rebating safety:** every version says "refer / when we quote them" —
  never "buy a policy and win." Do not let a designer or ad reword it to
  imply purchase.
- **Open dependencies:** (1) the live site must be redeployed from `main`
  before these links show the jackpot/rules correctly; (2) counsel sign-off
  on rules + anti-rebating before paid spend.
