# Operating Playbook — Wiley-Wilson Agency
### Running QuoteSync with a 3-person team

> **The one rule:** Protect Tracy's proactive time. A renewal *saved* is worth more than a phone *answered fast*. Everything below exists to keep Tracy working renewal cases instead of reacting to the phone.

---

## Who does what

| Role | Person | Their job in one line |
|---|---|---|
| **Front Desk** *(unlicensed — cousin)* | _____________ | **Keep Tracy's phone from ringing.** Answer every inbound call, resolve the clerical stuff, queue the rest, escalate true emergencies. |
| **Retention** *(licensed)* | **Tracy** | **Save renewals before the bill.** Work renewal cases proactively + the cross-sell on them. Clear service tasks in batches, never as interrupts. |
| **New Business + Growth** *(licensed)* | **You** | **Close.** Quote & bind new business, work your own renewals/cross-sell, take the hard saves. |

---

## Phone triage — the Front Desk decision tree

**Every inbound call → one of three buckets:**

- 🔴 **ESCALATE NOW → Tracy** — "I want to cancel," payment due *today*, just had a claim, upset/leaving.
  *These are saves. They can't wait.*
- 🟢 **I'll HANDLE IT** — take a payment, give existing-policy info, send ID cards, book an appointment, simple clerical update (address, lienholder, billing date), take a message.
  *Resolve it and log it.*
- 🟡 **QUEUE FOR TRACY** — anything that needs a license: a coverage change, *"what should I have / what would it cost,"* a question I can't answer.
  *Log it as a service task; Tracy batches it later.*

> **When unsure → Queue, don't Handle.** Never give coverage or price advice.

---

## Daily rhythm

**Front Desk:** phones all day · resolve clerical items as they come · keep the service-task queue current.

**Tracy:**
- **Morning — RENEWAL BLOCK (protected, phone off):** work the top renewal cases in the queue, ahead of the bill. Cross-sell flagged → hand to You.
- **Afternoon — SERVICE BATCH:** clear the licensed service tasks from the queue in one pass.
- **Late day:** callbacks & follow-ups.

**You:** new-business quoting/binding in defended blocks · check the queue for cross-sell hand-offs · run the weekly review.

---

## The Front Desk lane *(compliance — unlicensed)*

✅ Phones · payments · existing-policy info · scheduling · clerical data entry · messages
❌ **No** quoting · **no** rates · **no** coverage advice · **no** binding · **no** "what should you have"
*Those always route to Tracy or You.*

> Confirm the exact line with the **GA Dept. of Insurance** and **Allstate**. Consider a **limited CSR license** as she grows.

---

## What we measure *(not "calls answered")*

- **Tracy:** renewal saves + cross-sell hand-offs **↑** · reactive time **↓**
- **Front Desk:** % of inbound resolved **without** escalating to Tracy **↑**
- **Agency:** Net Retention trend on the QuoteSync scoreboard **↑**

---

## Weekly 20-minute check-in *(off the QuoteSync scoreboard)*

Renewal save rate · cross-sells surfaced · service queue cleared · anything stuck.
**If reactive load creeps back in — fix the triage rules, not the people.**

---
*The whole model in one breath: the Front Desk buys back Tracy's attention so she can do the one thing that compounds — proactive renewal saves and cross-sell — while You bring in new business. Shield the phone, resolve-or-queue everything reactive, batch what's left, and measure saves, not calls.*
