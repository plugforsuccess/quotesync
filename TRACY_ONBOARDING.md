# Welcome to QuoteSync, Tracy 👋

### Your guide to the retention desk — Wiley‑Wilson Agency

This is everything you need to run your day in QuoteSync. Read it once start to
finish, then keep it open for your first week. Nothing here is complicated — the
system is built to do one thing: **help you save renewals and cancellations
before the customer ever leaves.**

> **The one rule that matters most:** *A renewal you **save** is worth more than a
> phone you **answer fast.** Your job is proactive saves — protect that time.*

---

## 1. Who you are in this system

You are the **Retention** specialist. In one line:

> **Save renewals and pending cancellations before the bill — by phone, ahead of
> time, one protected block at a time.**

You're licensed, so you can do everything: talk coverage, talk price, make the
change. The system routes the at‑risk customers straight to **your** queue.

---

## 2. Getting in & what you'll see

1. Sign in at the agency login link (ask Cam for the bookmark).
2. You'll land on **your workspace**. Across the top you have four tabs:

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Today** | Your ranked to‑do list for the day + your call target. Start here. |
| **My Queue** | The dialer. Every at‑risk renewal & cancellation assigned to you. This is where you live. |
| **Service Batch** | Admin/service tasks to clear in one afternoon block (not phone interrupts). |
| **Scorecard** | How you're doing — saves, premium retained, premium saved. |

> If your queue looks empty on day one, it just means the latest Allstate
> reports haven't been uploaded yet — tell Cam. The cases flow to you
> automatically once they're imported.

---

## 3. Your daily rhythm

This is the shape of a good day. Defend the morning block above all.

**🌅 Morning — RENEWAL BLOCK (protected, phone to voicemail)**
Work the top of your **My Queue → Renewals**, ahead of the bill. This is your
highest‑value time. Don't answer the main line during this block — let it go to
voicemail and batch the callbacks later.

**🌞 Afternoon — SERVICE BATCH**
Open **Service Batch** and clear the licensed service tasks in one pass instead
of letting them interrupt you all day.

**🌆 Late day — CALLBACKS & FOLLOW‑UPS**
Work anyone you promised to call back (see §6).

---

## 4. How to work a case — the core loop

Everything happens in **My Queue**. It has two tabs: **Cancellations** (people
about to lose coverage) and **Renewals** (people whose policy is coming up).
Pick a tab, pick the top case, and:

**Step 1 — Call.** Click the green **📞 Call** button. It dials on your phone.

**Step 2 — Read the script.** Right under the call button you'll see a **Call
script** written for that exact customer (their name, product, date, and — for
cancellations — the balance). Read it naturally; it's a starting point, not a
cage.

**Step 3 — No answer? Leave the voicemail.** There's a **Voicemail** script
right below — read it word‑for‑word. It's written to be safe to overhear (no
balances or coverage details) and to give them a reason to call back.

**Step 4 — Open the case to record what happened.** Click **Open case →**. This
opens the full work surface where you:
- **Log the call** outcome (reached / no answer / left voicemail).
- **Schedule a callback** if they asked you to call later (see §6).
- Mark the outcome:
  - **Renewals:** *Confirmed Renewal ✓* (a save!) or *Lost — Won't Renew*.
  - **Cancellations:** *Saved*, *Rewritten*, or *Wants to Cancel*.
- Add a note to the **Case Log** (see §7).

> **Tip:** The list cards are intentionally lean — just **Call · Snooze · Open
> case**. All the "what happened" recording lives inside **Open case**, so your
> work is captured in one place.

---

## 5. When you save someone — capture the premium

This one's important and new. When you mark a renewal **Confirmed**, the work
surface asks for the **final premium the customer agreed to pay**.

Type it in. The system shows **"💰 Saved them $X — Y% off the renewal offer"** and
rolls it into your scorecard. This is how the agency sees the real dollars you're
saving — so always fill it in when you save someone.

---

## 6. Callbacks — schedule them, then find them

**To schedule:** inside a case (Open case → **Schedule a callback**), pick the
date/time and an optional note, then **📅 Schedule**.

**To find them later:** in **My Queue**, click the **📅 Callbacks** filter
(it's a chip on the Cancellations tab and a card on the Renewals tab). It shows
**everyone you owe a callback, soonest/overdue first.** Work that list in your
late‑day block.

> A scheduled callback shows on the case as **"📅 Call back {time}"**, and flips
> to **"⏰ overdue"** once the time passes — so nothing slips.

---

## 7. The Case Log — write it down

Every case has a **Case Log** (inside Open case). Type what happened and hit
**Add comment**. Notes are **permanent and stamped with your name** — they're the
memory of the account. Pick a category from the dropdown if it helps (Call
Summary, Follow‑up, Escalation…), but it's optional; **General** is fine.

Rule of thumb: **if you talked to them, log a one‑line summary.** Future‑you (and
Cam) will thank you.

---

## 8. Service Batch — the afternoon admin block

Some calls produce admin work (billing change, add a vehicle, ID cards, a
coverage question). Instead of doing it mid‑call and breaking your rhythm, it
gets logged as a **service task** and you clear them together in **Service
Batch**. They're grouped into three lanes, worked top to bottom:

1. **Coverage & Price** 🔒 — needs a licensed agent (you). Do these first, while fresh.
2. **Policy Changes** 🔒 — vehicle, mortgage company, billing, address.
3. **Quick Tasks** — ID cards, documents, confirmations. Close out last.

Each task has a **Copy for Allstate** button and a 24‑hour clock — the goal is to
finish every service task within a day.

---

## 9. Your scorecard — how you're measured

Open **Scorecard** any time. We measure **saves, not calls answered**:
- **Renewal save rate** and **premium retained**
- **💰 Premium saved** (the dollars you negotiated off renewals)
- **Cancellation save rate** and **premium at risk saved**

If reactive phone work starts creeping into your morning block, that's a process
problem to fix with Cam — not a you problem.

---

## 10. Clocking in & out

Use the shared **Time Clock kiosk** — enter your 4‑digit PIN to clock in, out,
and for lunch. (Ask Cam for the kiosk link and your PIN.) It greets you by name
and shows your hours for the day.

---

## 11. The golden rules

✅ **Do**
- Protect your morning renewal block — phone to voicemail.
- Call first, read the script, log every call.
- Capture the **saved premium** every time you save a renewal.
- Schedule callbacks and clear them from the **Callbacks** filter.
- Work **Service Batch** in one afternoon block.

🚫 **Don't**
- Don't let the phone pull you out of proactive work all day.
- Don't resolve a case from the list without opening it — that's where the
  outcome and saved premium get captured.
- Don't reassign your own cases (that's a manager action).
